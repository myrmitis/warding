Every live session gets a pixel character.

Agent Worlds is a page in the dashboard where each running agent session is an animated sprite at a desk, named after its task. Seven scenes, one setting, and it works with whichever harness you dock.

The Office, running

Simulated demo · real renderer · your time

The Night Office. Simulated demo: the sprites follow a scripted night, drawn by a port of the dashboard's Office renderer. Pause stops it; with reduced motion it holds one still frame.

Seven scenes

  • Office

    The classic pixel office: one desk per session, a wall clock, and a task board.

  • Panda Den

    A bamboo-forest workspace where every agent is a panda.

  • Neural Net

    Sessions as bright nodes on a constellation map.

  • Wizard Tower

    An alchemy lab, for the sessions that brew.

  • Deep Lab

    An underwater research station, porthole light included.

  • Mission Control

    An operations room of consoles, one per live session.

  • Watering Hole

    A savanna with giraffes, warthogs and elephants.

Switch scenes from the tabs at the top of the Agent Worlds page; the choice is remembered in that browser.

What a sprite tells you

Each sprite is one session, labelled with its task and its message count. A session that is working looks busy at its desk; an idle one sits. A tool call waiting on your approval shows as an overlay on the scene, with Approve and Deny, and the answer goes through the same approval path as the dashboard and your chat channels.

A dedicated hand-raised pose for a waiting sprite is not drawn yet. In development

The picture beside this is the dashboard itself, with four seeded sessions and no agent connected. No recorded night stands behind it, and the caption says so.

Save tonight as a wallpaper

The button under the frame above saves a PNG of this page's simulated frame at twice its size, stamped with your local time. It is a feature of this website, drawn from the scripted scene, not an export of your own sessions.

In the dashboard, the same export from your live world is in development. In development

The Vault

An eighth scene, drawn from scratch for the people who fund this work, with a written provenance line for every sprite and tile. It does not exist yet, and nothing is sold on the promise of it. In development
