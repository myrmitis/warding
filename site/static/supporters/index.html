How a name gets here

  1. You become a Founding Supporter on the pricing page (a free waitlist until the tier opens).
  2. At checkout you choose whether your name appears on this wall. The default is off. The numbered seal badge in your dashboard is yours either way.
  3. Opted-in names are listed in the order they joined, with the seal number and the month. No photos, no amounts, no company logos.
  4. Change your mind later and the line is removed after a reviewed export and manifest update. Nothing else about you is published.

What the money is for

A numbered seal badge in your dashboard and, if you opt in, on the Supporters wall; The Vault Agent World scene; the Charter Paper and Warding Night theme pack; a Discord role; a roadmap vote. None of it exists yet, so nothing is sold: the tier opens when every item on this card ships.
